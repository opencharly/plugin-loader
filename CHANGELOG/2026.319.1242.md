## Summary

`plugin-loader` implements `spec.ProjectLoader.ValidateEntityCUEWithGates` — the seam method that carries the host's threaded kind gates into entity validation.

Two constraints meet at this one-line delegate, and it is the only place they can be satisfied together:

1. **A kind whose authored body moved into a plugin validates only through its declared gate.** The base table's entry for `vm` is `#ResolvedVm`, the **post-default envelope** (`autostart`, `firmware`, `backend` all required), which no authored document can satisfy — that is charly's `autostart: field is required but not present` red (opencharly/opencharly#1048).
2. **The host may not import the sdk mechanism kits.** charly's import-purity gate forbids `github.com/opencharly/sdk/loaderkit` in core, so the host reaches the capability over its loader seam — and this plugin is the loader.

The delegate follows its ungated sibling exactly: one call into `sdk/loaderkit`, which is where the mechanism lives and is tested (`sdk#527`, including the `nil`-gates control).

Pins: `spec v0.2026319.1226` (the method's declaration, `spec#329`) and `sdk v0.2026319.1126` (`sdk#527`, the resolution).
